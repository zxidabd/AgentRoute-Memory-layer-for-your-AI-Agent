# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) supplements the MemoryBrain Terms of Service entered into by and between **MemoryBrain Inc.** (Processor or MemoryBrain) and the Customer (Controller).

---

## 1. Scope and Applicability
This DPA applies to the processing of Personal Data by MemoryBrain on behalf of Customer in connection with providing the MemoryBrain Long-Term Memory Layer for AI Agents.

## 2. Processing Obligations
1. **Instructions:** MemoryBrain shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including with respect to transfers of data outside the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland.
2. **Confidentiality:** MemoryBrain ensures that persons authorized to process personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality.
3. **Security:** MemoryBrain implements technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:
   - AES-256 field-level encryption of memory contents at rest.
   - TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit.
   - Strict logical multi-tenant database isolation.
   - Continuous compliance monitoring and immutable audit logging.

## 3. Subprocessors
1. Customer grants general written authorization to MemoryBrain to engage subprocessors to perform specific processing activities.
2. An up-to-date list of subprocessors is maintained at https://docs.memorybrain.ai/trust/subprocessors.
3. MemoryBrain shall notify Customer at least 30 days prior to adding or replacing any subprocessor.

## 4. Data Subject Rights & Assistance
1. MemoryBrain provides automated self-serve mechanisms (DELETE /v1/users/{user_id}) enabling Customer to fulfill Data Subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, and portability).
2. MemoryBrain shall notify Customer without undue delay upon receiving any request directly from a Data Subject.

## 5. Security Incidents & Breach Notification
MemoryBrain shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within **72 hours**, of becoming aware of any confirmed Personal Data Breach impacting Customer data.

## 6. Data Deletion and Return
Upon termination of the Services, MemoryBrain shall, at Customer's election, return or permanently delete all Customer Personal Data within 30 days, unless applicable law requires continued storage.
